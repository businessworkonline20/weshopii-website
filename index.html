Common questions

Things store owners ask us

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. They message you on WhatsApp, tap your store link, and order in their browser. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

Where does the money go?

Straight into your own UPI account, instantly, with the notification you already know. There's no middleman holding it and no commission taken out.

Do I need a computer?

No. Everything runs from your phone - taking orders, adding products with your camera, updating prices and stock.

What if I already use billing software?

You can export your product list from almost any billing software and upload it in one go, so you don't have to type everything again.

Who sets everything up?

We do. We build your product list, print your counter QR code, and walk you through it in person. You start taking orders the same day.

Our story

We think the corner store deserves to win

The shops that have served their neighbourhoods for decades are quietly losing customers to apps that take a cut and keep the relationship. We didn't think that was inevitable - so we built the other option.

" Your customer is yours. Your money is yours. Your store stays yours.

The problem

A store owner watches footfall shrink as regulars start ordering from apps - apps that take a fifth of every sale and never let the store see who's actually buying.

Our answer

Give every store its own online store, on the app their customers already use - with no commission, payments direct to their UPI, and full ownership of every relationship.

Hyperlocal by design

Each store serves its own two or three kilometres, with its own delivery. Not a marketplace, not a warehouse network - just a trusted local store, brought online.
